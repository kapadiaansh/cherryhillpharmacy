Our services

Everyday pharmacy care, tailored to you and your family.

We can prescribe for eligible minor ailments — health conditions that can be managed with minimal treatment and self-care. Learn more at OCP's minor ailment page.

We provide a comprehensive range of compounding services to meet your unique medication needs, prepared by our expert compounding pharmacists.

Having trouble organizing your medication? We package it into easy-to-use compliance packs, so it's simpler to track doses, refills and any changes.

Seniors save 10% every day on over-the-counter (OTC) products, and 20% every Tuesday. Ask in-store to confirm current promotion details.

Our trained pharmacists administer flu, COVID-19, travel and other eligible vaccines in-store. Book ahead for quicker service — walk-ins are also welcome.

Available to every patient, our medication reviews help you understand your regimen, catch drug-therapy issues early and improve your overall health outcomes.

Too busy to stop by? We deliver prescriptions free, anywhere in London, right to your door — so you never miss a dose.

Moving your prescriptions to Cherryhill Pharmacy is easy. Give us a call and we'll take care of the rest.

Our team works efficiently while keeping things warm and personal, tailoring care so every visit is a good one.

We accept all drug plans to fill your prescriptions — just bring your card in or give us a call.
