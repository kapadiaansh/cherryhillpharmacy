OUR SERVICES

We can now prescribe for Minor Ailments. Minor Ailments are health conditions that can be managed with minimal treatment and/or self-care strategies. For more information, please visit OCP's minor ailment website .
Cherryhill Pharmacy provides a comprehensive range of compounding services to meet your unique medication needs. Our expert compounding pharmacists are committed to delivering high-quality compounded medications.
Having trouble organizing your medication? Let us do it for you! We can package your medication in an easy-to-use package that makes it easier than sorting through your prescription bottles. It also helps you keep track of medications, refills, and medication changes.
We offer 10% Seniors Discounts EVERYDAY on over-the-counter (OTC) products at Cherryhill Pharmacy.

Enjoy the Special 20% Seniors Discount every Tuesday on ALL over-the-counter (OTC) products!

Our trained pharmacists are available to administer Flu, COVID-19, Travel vaccines, and other prescribed vaccines in-store. Please book your appointment in advance to ensure quick and convenient service. Walk-ins are also welcome!
We offer medication reviews for all our patients as part of our patient-care services. These reviews aim to enhance your understanding of your medication regimen, identify and resolve drug therapy issues, and improve your overall health outcomes.
Is it inconvenient coming in for your refills? No worries. We offer free prescription delivery anywhere in London, right to your door, for your convenience.
Transferring your prescriptions to Cherryhill Pharmacy is easy. Just give us a call, and we will handle the rest.
At our pharmacy, we are committed to providing fast, friendly, and personalized service. Our dedicated team works efficiently to meet your needs while offering a warm and welcoming atmosphere. We tailor our approach to ensure you receive the best care and attention.
We accept all drug plans to fill your prescriptions.
